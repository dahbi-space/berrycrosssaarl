Quality

Quality You Can Trust

Every batch is grown under GlobalG.A.P. good agricultural practices and GRASP social-compliance standards, with full traceability from field to dispatch. Fruit is graded, inspected, and packed to export specification under strict quality control.

Logistics

Cold Chain & Export Logistics

Blackberries are delicate, so they are pre-cooled within hours of harvest and held in temperature-controlled conditions through grading, packing, and transit. We handle export-ready documentation and ship by air, sea, or road, keeping the cold chain intact to your destination.

Counter-seasonal advantage

Available When You Need It Most

Morocco's blackberry season runs from late autumn through early summer, filling the supply gap when European domestic production is at its lowest. Sourcing from Morocco gives buyers consistent, high-quality volumes during the off-season window — with shorter transit times to Europe than overseas origins.
