— FAQ

Buyer questions, answered

What are your minimum order volumes?+

We supply from a single pallet for trial orders up to full reefer and air-freight programmes. Minimums vary by product and destination — we will confirm against your spec in the quote.

Which Incoterms do you offer?+

EXW, FOB and CIF are standard, with DAP available to most European destinations. We will recommend the most efficient routing for your market.

Can you supply private-label packaging?+

Yes. We pack to your specification — retail punnets, clamshells or bulk — with your branding, barcodes and labelling for the destination market.

How do you guarantee the cold chain?+

Fruit is pre-cooled within the first hour of harvest and held in a temperature-logged chain through packing, storage and transit. Data loggers travel with each consignment.

Which certifications do you hold?+

GlobalG.A.P., GRASP, BRCGS, ISO 22000 and HACCP, with full lot-level traceability documentation available on request.

Which markets do you ship to?+

Western Europe, the United Kingdom, Scandinavia and the Gulf, with new corridors added as volumes grow.
