Moroccan avocados are available from September through April, with Hass typically peaking from October to March. Morocco's proximity to European markets means shorter transit times and a fresher product compared to origins from South America or South Africa. Contact us for current availability and crop maturity updates.

Maturity & ripening

Picked at the Right Maturity

Avocados don't ripen on the tree, so harvest timing and dry-matter content are critical. We pick at the correct maturity and can supply fruit firm (unripe) for your own ripening programme, giving you control over shelf-ready timing at destination.

Quality

Quality You Can Trust

Every batch is grown under GlobalG.A.P. good agricultural practices and GRASP social-compliance standards, with full traceability from field to dispatch. Fruit is calibrated, inspected, and packed to export specification under strict quality control.

Logistics

Cold Chain & Export Logistics

Avocados are pre-cooled after harvest and held in temperature-controlled conditions through grading, packing, and transit. We handle export-ready documentation and ship by sea or road in refrigerated containers, keeping the cold chain intact to your destination.
