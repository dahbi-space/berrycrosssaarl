Sourcing Advantage

Why Source Fresh Produce from Morocco?

Morocco's geography and growing calendar make it one of Europe's most strategic fresh-produce origins — counter-seasonal supply, short logistics routes, and competitive pricing that holds up at scale.

01

Counter-Seasonal Supply Window

Moroccan berry and avocado seasons run from autumn through spring, filling the gap when Northern European production goes offline. Your supply chain stays active year-round without relying on expensive southern-hemisphere origins.

02

Short Transit Times to Europe

Morocco reaches major European distribution centres in 2–4 days by refrigerated road freight, or via regular reefer sea routes for larger volumes. Less time in transit means better arrival quality and fewer quality claims.

03

Ideal Climate & Growing Regions

The Souss-Massa, Gharb, and Loukkos regions combine abundant Atlantic sunshine, mild winters, and managed irrigation — producing fruit that's consistently sweet, firm, and graded to export specification season after season.

04

Competitive Wholesale Pricing

Lower input costs translate into attractive FOB and CIF prices for importers and distributors, giving you a cost advantage over equivalent produce from longer-haul origins without compromising on GlobalG.A.P. certified standards.

Quality & Compliance

Export Standards You Can Rely On

Every BerryCross shipment meets the compliance requirements of professional import markets. Our supply base holds GlobalG.A.P. and GRASP certification — independently audited assurance covering food safety, environmental responsibility, and worker welfare.

Export documentation — phytosanitary certificates, certificates of origin, health certificates, and EUR.1 movement certificates — is coordinated in-house. Our logistics team works directly with cold-chain carriers to ensure your cargo travels within specification, from the packing room to your destination port.

  • ✓ GlobalG.A.P. certified — the EU market standard for food safety, traceability, and responsible growing practices
  • ✓ GRASP certified — risk-assessed social practices on our supply farms, required by leading European retailers
  • ✓ Full farm-to-dock traceability — lot-level tracking across harvest, packing, and transport for every shipment
  • ✓ Complete export documentation — phytosanitary, health certificates, EUR.1 movement certificate, and certificates of origin
  • ✓ Cold-chain monitored throughout — pre-cooling post-harvest, temperature-controlled packing rooms, reefer transport with logs available on request
